# Quick Start Guide

Get Content Next integrated into your Next.js application in just a few steps. This guide will walk you through everything from setting up your account to displaying articles on your site.

## What You'll Build

By the end of this guide, you'll have:

- ✅ A Content Next account and website project
- ✅ API key configured in your Next.js app
- ✅ A blog listing page showing all articles
- ✅ Individual article pages with full content
- ✅ Analytics tracking for views and engagement

## Prerequisites

Before you begin, make sure you have:

- **Next.js 14+** with App Router
- **Node.js 18+** or Bun
- A **Content Next account** (sign up at [content-next.com](https://content-next.com))

## Time Estimate

This guide takes approximately **5-10 minutes** to complete.
