# Analytics Tracking

Content Next includes built-in analytics to help you understand how your articles are performing. Track page views, reading time, scroll depth, and more with a simple script tag.

## What Gets Tracked

Content Next automatically tracks the following metrics:

### Page Views

- Total number of times an article page is loaded
- Unique visitors (based on browser cookies)
- View timestamps

### Reading Engagement

- **Read Time**: How long visitors spend on the article page (in seconds)
- **Scroll Depth**: How far down the page visitors scroll (0-100%)
- **Completion Rate**: Percentage of the article that was read

All analytics data is available in your Content Next dashboard where you can:

- View article performance over time
- Compare articles by engagement
- Identify your most popular content
- Track reading patterns

## Privacy-Focused

Content Next analytics are designed with privacy in mind:

- No personal data collected
- Anonymous visitor IDs only
- No third-party data sharing
- GDPR compliant
